ABOUT ME

I'm Zalfa Alya Tsaqifa, Taekwondo Assistant Coach

My journey in taekwondo began when I was in the first grade of elementary school. Through extensive experience as a competitive athlete, I have earned approximately 25 medals and 2 Best Athlete trophies across various tournaments.

Today, I channel that lifelong dedication into coaching, serving as an Assistant Coach at Taebo Bandung Club for the past 4 years, alongside mentoring students as an extracurricular school coach. I am committed to guiding the next generation of competitors toward excellence, both on and off the mat.
